# Systems Audit — Root Cause Analysis (2026-07-29)

**Trigger:** explicit user request to stop fixing spot issues and instead "zoom out" — fan out across the harness, map failure modes per subsystem, find how they cascade, and conclusively determine what's costing the biggest performance/quality gaps across all model tiers.

**Method:** 3-stage [[../../../Architecture/Specs/09-UNIFIED-PROGRAM|Workflow]]-orchestrated audit against `main`:

1. **6 parallel subsystem audits** (read-only, source + trace + wiki-report grounded) — one per system, each independently graded for evidence quality in the synthesis (§4 of [[07-synthesis]]):
   - [[01-tool-exposure]] — lazy disclosure, tool-relevance classifier, discover-tools, builtins floor, `tool-surface.ts` resolver
   - [[02-context-assembly]] — `packages/reasoning/src/assembly/*`, context-engine, Anthropic caching
   - [[03-error-handling]] — retry, M4 healing pipeline, verify/fix loops, LLM-call error handling
   - [[04-loop-control]] — `packages/reasoning/src/kernel/loop/`, arbitrator, low_delta_guard, strategy-switching, 8 registered strategies
   - [[05-cross-tier]] — capability/calibration system vs. measured haiku/qwen3.5/granite4 behavior
   - [[06-observability]] — RunLedger, sub-agent ledger merge (Wave C.2 slice 2), cross-provider token/cost accounting
2. **Single-agent (opus) cross-cutting synthesis** — [[07-synthesis]]. Independently re-verified load-bearing claims against source before writing (not just trusting the 6 audits), found 5 cascades linking findings across subsystem boundaries, surfaced 3 defects none of the 6 individual audits caught, and ranked a top-8 root-cause list.
3. **Adversarial verification** — [[08-verify]]. One independent skeptic agent per ranked root cause, instructed to default to `refuted=true` on failure to confirm. **Result: 8/8 confirmed, 0 refuted**, with corrections/nuances on 3 of the 8 (see table below). Ran three times due to session-limit/transient-error attrition on the original 24-agent (8×3-lens) design; final pass was a cost-downsized 8-agent single-lens design, with 11 additional single-lens confirmations recovered for free from the two earlier dead runs' workflow journals (zero extra tokens spent recovering them).

## Overall trends

**State of the harness: "structural."** Genuinely strong parts exist — the ledger substrate, compaction's protected-classes design, tool-surface resolver's ordered pipeline, the retry policy itself (where wired), `completion-status.ts`'s honesty rule ("shipping a partial is fine, calling it completed is not") — several better than what most production harnesses ship. But the defects are not independent: they're one architectural incoherence expressing itself repeatedly, plus a process pattern of shipping fixes only to the paths currently under measurement rather than to every path that structurally does the same thing:

- **The incoherence:** the harness decides whether the world changed by reasoning over a *reconstruction* of what it believes it did — ledger entries, recorded tool-call args, authorship heuristics, LLM-inferred required-tool sets — and never checks the world. Produces an 88% false-failure rate across 3 tiers on real correct deliverables, a guaranteed false-negative on code-action delegation, and a benchmark script that had to write `// Grade on DISK, not on the model's claim about the disk` to measure its own product honestly.
- **The process pattern — "fixed where we were looking":** Wave C.2 wired 3 of 4 delegation sites, missing the 4th (root cause #7). F6 landed the classifier fix at the resolver but not the config layer that actually gates it, and a test then *pinned the gap as correct* (root cause #2). The token-accounting sweep (D-2026-07-28-A) covered 3 of 4 providers, missing LiteLLM. The local-tier driver's Stage B was never built while its target models (qwen3.5, granite4) became the project's own local benchmark targets (root cause #4). Local tier's compensations (root cause #8) are all inert for the identical reason.
- **A new species of instrument fault** (synthesis §2b): rung-2-haiku-composite.md's own claim that "cost is near-deterministic at n=3" is falsified by its own raw per-run data (2× cost spread within one arm, driven by cache-hit and iteration-count variance). Unlike every prior instrument fault this project caught this cycle, this one is *not* a broken probe — it's a correct probe paired with an overconfident read of its own noise floor.
- **A loop-control budget leak** (synthesis §2c): a benchmark script's declared `.withMaxIterations(12)` is silently multiplied by strategy-switching's pass count, since the loop guard is per-kernel-pass, not per-run — recorded haiku cells ran 16–28 iterations against a declared cap of 12.
- **Process miss in the audit itself:** loop-control and cross-tier each held half of what became Cascade B (the fragmented success authority) and neither had both, because subsystem-based decomposition cut across a defect that spans termination + verification + receipts + cross-tier data. Worth remembering the next time subsystem boundaries are chosen for a fan-out audit.

**Two cheapest, highest-leverage fixes** (synthesis §5): one commit giving the success authority a single owner with access to ground truth (fixes root causes #1 and the D-2026-07-28-D false-positive risk together — fixing either alone trades one error direction for the other); one commit removing the classifier's silent default so the *next* measurement session measures the system instead of a confound (root cause #2). Until the second lands, every 2026-07-28 arm comparison in [[../../Harness-Reports/2026-07-28-rung1-flag-inertness|rung1]]/[[../../Harness-Reports/2026-07-28-rung2-haiku-composite|rung2]]/rung3 reports — including F8, currently ranked #1 in the failure-mode catalogue — is provisional.
