Predictions and kill criteria: [[PREDICTIONS]]. Full tables: `s4-tables.md`. ## Verdict: PASS (method works; two refinements)

Kill criteria not met: 2,866 unique traces resolve and parse (of 3,203 saved cells; 3,144 carry a trace path; the rest were pruned or lived in temp dirs), covering 20 tasks and 12 tiers; every named mechanism maps to keys. Refinements: (1) the activity key means "the mechanism spoke", not "it changed the run" (pth, below); (2) four comparisons (bat-custom, bat-h3mi2, fam-nofam, fam-nocon) have no resolvable traces, so their mechanisms (budgetAnswerTurn, named-family disclosure, call-constraint notes) have no census row.

## "Fired" is not "acted": the pth example

pth fires in 100% of its cells, so by the trigger-rate bar it is not a rare mechanism. But the behaviour change needs the model to have independent calls to batch. From the speedups receipt (`wiki/Research/Ablations/2026-11-18-speedups/results/table.md`), multi-call turns appear on todo-heavy, exact-value and golden-moons only; on todo-heavy cogito turns go 13 to 3 and tokens 16,974 to 4,529, on the other tasks nothing moves. Whole-suite geo-mean token ratio is 0.85 with a CI of [0.47, 1.57] (S3). The activity key says "the hint rendered"; the proximal trigger is "a turn with >= 2 independent tool calls". The latter is a raw-event reader (`tool-call-start` per iteration, as `analyze.ts` already does), not an activity key. Recommend each mechanism pre-registers a proximal-trigger predicate, and the census reads it from traces.

## Dilution

For a whole-suite lift W at corpus trigger rate r, effect-given-trigger must be W / r. The table in `s4-tables.md` has it per mechanism. Examples at W = +3 pp: dispatcher_early_stop ending 78 pp; strategy switching 70 pp; act-or-answer forcing 93 pp; tool gate 121 pp (impossible); loop_guidance 614 pp (impossible); parallelToolHint and leanFinalTurn corpus-wide 179-191 pp (impossible), but in-arm they fire on 83-100% of cells, which is the point: **trigger rate must be measured in the arm and per task, not suite-wide.** Mechanisms that fire in < 5% of all cells and sit on one or two tasks (plan-fanout-split: 1 task; loop_guidance; progress_aware_exit; ri-early-stop; required_tool_quota: 3 tasks) cannot be read from a whole-suite A/B at n = 5; they need prefix-fork probes or targeted-task cells.

## Caveats

- Corpus mixes arms, code versions and tiers; per-key rates drift with what was archived. Per-task rates for `<5%` keys come from 1-3 tasks, so they are leads for targeted cells, not stable estimates.
- 91% of path-bearing cells resolved; pruned traces bias toward recent runs.
- Key families reuse `mechanismActivity` verbatim, so the census inherits its blind spots: it sees guards, signals, switches and requirements, not model-compliance events.
