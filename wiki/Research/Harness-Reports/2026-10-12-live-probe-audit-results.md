## What was NOT reproduced / refined

- **FM-A1-style answer-loss on qwen3:4b agentic runs**: the two qwen3:4b traces rotated out mid-session; what was captured showed normal FC content lengths (91-469 chars) — the thinking-loss harm manifests most on *long* reasoning turns (raw probe: content=2 chars), i.e. hardest tasks, not trivial ones.
- **"14.5KB default FC payload"** — refined to: 11.8KB for the opt-in builtin set; the truly DEFAULT zero-extra-tools agent is small (live trace: 1 tool). The finding holds for the realistic SLM-agent config (file/terminal/MCP tools), not the empty builder.
- **`predictNumCtx` (D-2026-07-30-I)**: already half-wired and deliberately kept opt-in after measurement (model-reload cost 33-49s/switch) — not a free win; leave as-is.
- Loop-detector W8 plumbing is LIVE (switch did fire once in the trace); the gap is specifically the **success-shaped, varied-args, zero-evidence-delta** thrash class, confirmed both patterns-math and live ledger.

## Live-run impact summary (the case for the program)

On a 27B local thinking model (qwen3.5), the framework's own trace shows a task achievable in 2 calls consumed 10 iterations, produced zero evidence delta throughout, was mislabeled "green" pace, ended by a zero-confidence early-stop **while acting**, and was declared **success** without delivering the contract requirement — all while its assessment substrate HAD computed the correct diagnosis every iteration. The signals exist; the actuators are dark. See [[Planning/Implementation-Plans/2026-10-12-local-tier-reliability-control-program]] for the fix plan.
